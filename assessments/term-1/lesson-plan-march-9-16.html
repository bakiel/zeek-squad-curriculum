Monday, 9 March 2026
ELA Assessment 40 Questions
9:00 AM - Session Opens (5 min)
Say exactly this to open the session:
"Shalom everyone! Welcome to Zeek Squad. Today is a special day - we are going to do our ELA Assessment. This means we check how much you have learned! Remember, this is not a test to scare you - it is just to show how much you KNOW. Do your best and you will do great. Parents, please have paper and pencil ready for your child. Let's begin!"
  • Confirm all children are present on Zoom
  • Ask parents to have the assessment sheet ready (printed or on screen)
  • Open the ELA Assessment file from Google Classroom
9:05 AM - Section 1: Letters (Questions 1-10) | 10 min
Script - Letters Section:
"Let's start with our letters! I will read each question. You circle or write your answer on your sheet. Ready? Let's go!"

Read each question slowly and clearly:

  • Q1: "Question 1. Circle the UPPERCASE letter. The choices are: a, B, c, d."
  • Q2: "Question 2. Circle the LOWERCASE letter. The choices are: F, G, h, J."
  • Q3: "What letter comes BEFORE D? Write it on your paper."
  • Q4: "What letter comes AFTER M? Write it on your paper."
  • Q5-10: Continue reading each question at a steady pace
Tip: Pause for 10-15 seconds after each question before reading the next one.
9:15 AM - Section 2: Vowels (Questions 11-20) | 10 min
Script - Vowels Section:
"Great work! Now we move to vowels. Remember - A, E, I, O, U are our special vowel letters. Listen carefully to each question."

Read questions 11-20. For sound questions, say the word clearly and slowly.

For Q16 (vowel in "BIG"): say the word slowly: B - I - G. Emphasise the middle sound.
9:25 AM - Section 3: ABC Order (Questions 21-30) | 10 min
Script - ABC Order Section:
"Now we are going to put letters in ABC order! There is a help strip on your paper with the whole alphabet. You may use it for questions 21 to 25. For questions 26 to 30, try to remember on your own!"

Read each set of letters clearly. Spell them out: "The letters are G... B... D. Put them in ABC order."

9:35 AM - Section 4 and 5: Rhyming and Sight Words (Questions 31-40) | 10 min
Script - Rhyming:
"Rhyming words sound the same at the end! CAT and HAT rhyme. DOG and LOG rhyme. Listen to my question..."
Script - Sight Words (Q36-40):
"Now I am going to show you a word. I want you to read it aloud to your parent. Your parent will circle YES if you can read it. Ready? The word is..."

Hold up each sight word card or display it on screen. Give children 5-10 seconds to read aloud to their parent.

9:45 AM - Collect and Close (5 min)
Script - Closing:
"Excellent work today! You are all ELA superstars! Parents - please take a clear photo of the completed assessment sheet and send it to me today. We will give everyone a star for doing their best. See you tomorrow for MATH! Shalom!"
  • Remind parents to send assessment photos today
  • Record who was present
  • Note any children who needed extra help

Tuesday, 10 March 2026
Math Assessment 40 Questions
9:00 AM - Session Opens (5 min)
Opening script:
"Shalom Zeek Squad! Today is MATH day! I love math because numbers are everywhere - in nature, in food, in music! You have been practising all term and today you get to show how much you know. Parents - children may use their FINGERS to count. That is perfectly fine. Ready? Let's go!"
9:05 AM - Section 1 and 2: Numbers and Counting (Q1-20) | 20 min

Numbers section (Q1-10): Read each number clearly. For "write the number THREE" - say both the word AND the number: "Write the number three - that is 3."

Counting section (Q11-20): For dot questions, count with the children: "Let's count the dots together: 1, 2, 3... How many did you count? Write that number."

For Q14 and Q15 (draw shapes): say "Parents, please give your child space to draw on their paper."
Script for Q29 (word problem):
"Listen carefully. I have 3 apples. My friend gives me 4 more. How many apples do I have now? Say it out loud - 3... plus 4 more... write your answer."
9:25 AM - Section 3 and 4: Addition and Subtraction (Q21-40) | 20 min
Script for Addition:
"Now we add numbers together! I will say the sum. You write the answer. You can use your fingers! Ready? Question 21. Two... plus... three... equals what? Write your answer."
Script for Subtraction:
"Now we TAKE AWAY numbers! I will say the sum and you write the answer. Question 31. Five... TAKE AWAY... two... equals what?"
Say "plus" for addition and "take away" or "minus" for subtraction. Both are acceptable.
9:45 AM - Collect and Close (5 min)
Closing script:
"Amazing work, Math champions! You worked so hard today. Parents - please send us a photo of the completed assessment today. Tomorrow we have our HEBREW and SCIENCE assessments. Tell your children to sing the Days of the Week tonight to get ready! Shalom!"

Wednesday, 11 March 2026
Hebrew + Science Assessments Written + Oral 20 Questions
9:00 AM - Hebrew Assessment: Written (Q1-15) | 25 min
Opening script:
"Shalom! Today we start with HEBREW! You have been learning the first letters of the aleph-bet. Let's see how much you remember. The letters we are testing are: ALEPH - that is the first letter. BET - that makes the B sound. And VET - that makes the V sound. Ready?"

Written section flow:

  • Q1-5: Hold up or display each Hebrew letter. Ask children to name it.
  • Q6-7: "Now we trace. Parents - help your child trace carefully over the light letters."
  • Q8-9: "Now write it on your OWN - no tracing this time!"
  • Q11-15: Read each knowledge question aloud. These are multiple choice.
9:25 AM - Hebrew Assessment: Oral Song (5 min)
Oral Song Script:
"Wonderful writing! Now it is time for the ORAL section. I want to hear you SING the Hebrew Days of the Week! Sing it with me first, then I will listen to each child individually. Ready? Let's sing together: Yom Rishon, Yom Sheni, Yom Shlishi, Yom Revi'i, Yom Chamishi, Yom Shishi, Shabbat! Great! Now let's hear each child sing it. [Child's name] - your turn!"
If a child is shy: "You can whisper it to your parent and they will tell us what you said. That counts!"

Mark the oral score on the Hebrew Assessment sheet (1-5 marks based on how many days they sing correctly).

9:30 AM - Science Assessment (Q1-20) | 25 min
Transition script:
"Brilliant singing! Now we switch to SCIENCE! Get your Science Assessment paper. This one is special - some questions ask you to DRAW a picture. Your drawings do not have to be perfect - just show me you understand!"

Key notes for science questions:

  • Q4, Q5: Drawing questions - full marks for ANY recognisable drawing
  • Q8: Drawing a plant - award marks for each part they label correctly
  • Q12: "Look outside with your parent right now - what is the weather doing?"
  • Q14: Drawing weather - encourage creativity
  • Q19: Drawing senses on a person - any figure drawing with labels is full marks
9:55 AM - Close (5 min)
Closing script:
"You have done THREE assessments this week - ELA, Math, and today Hebrew and Science! I am SO proud of every single one of you. Tomorrow is our PLANT PRESENTATION day. Each child will tell us about their plant project. Practise tonight! Parents - please send photos of today's two assessment sheets. Shalom!"

Thursday, 12 March 2026
Plant Project Presentations Each Child Presents
9:00 AM - Opening (5 min)
Opening script:
"Shalom Zeek Squad! Today is the BEST day of assessment week - PLANT PRESENTATION day! You have been growing and observing your plants all term. Today you get to be the TEACHER and tell us what you learned. Remember - there are no wrong answers. We want to hear YOUR observations and YOUR discoveries!"
9:05 AM - Presentations (40 min)

Each child gets 3-5 minutes to present. Use this structure:

Introduce each child:
"Everyone, please give a big clap for [Child's Name]! Tell us about your plant project. Start by showing us your plant or your drawings."

Prompt questions if the child is stuck:

  • "What did you plant? What is it called?"
  • "What did you give your plant every day?"
  • "What changes did you notice in your plant?"
  • "Can you name any parts of your plant?" (roots, stem, leaves, flower)
  • "What was the most interesting thing you discovered?"
After each presentation:
"Wonderful job [Name]! Let's all give [him/her] a big hand clap! Who learned something from [Name's] presentation? Raise your hand..."
If a child's plant did not survive: "That is SCIENCE! Plants teach us even when things do not go as planned. What did you learn from that experience? That is just as valuable!"
9:45 AM - Assessment Week Celebration (10 min)
Celebration script:
"Zeek Squad - you have COMPLETED your Term 1 assessments! ELA... Math... Hebrew... Science... AND Plant Presentations! You are incredible learners. Your teacher is going to review all your work and come back with wonderful feedback. Before we go - let's do our Zeek Squad cheer! Ready? ZEEK SQUAD IS THE BEST! We LEARN, we GROW, we DO NOT REST! Amazing! Have a beautiful day. Shalom!"
  • Thank parents for supporting their children this week
  • Remind parents to send any outstanding assessment photos
  • Note all children who presented and any observations
